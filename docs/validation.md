The collector accounts for all 181 in-window rows in Commons Library index
[SN04588](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/sn04588/)
and reconciles all 181 dated entries in its predecessor
[SN04587](https://researchbriefings.files.parliament.uk/documents/SN04587/SN04587.pdf),
including its explicit no-speech entry. Coverage is 180/181 (99.4%) of the
modern index. This measures coverage against the index. No independent
exhaustive search of all sittings or statistical precision estimate was made.

Verified entries have an anchored official Hansard transcript, a resolved
identity and constituency, and a cited party at the speech date. Party
labels come from the older speech index except where a documented source
supplies the party. The validator checks annual dates, chamber, source
anchors, unique speech IDs, required metadata, counts, text hashes, word
counts, and reconciliation with the two index totals.

## Source decisions

- Joan Quennell spoke on 31 May 1961. Both indexes give July, but Hansard
  gives May and the indexed volume and columns agree. Hansard transcribes
  the names of Quennell and James Tinn as Qnennell and Tina respectively.
  The source spellings are retained.
- Hansard gives 18 February 1964 for David Anderson and 17 June 1964 for
  Morgan Morgan-Giles. The older index gives 8 February and 7 June.
  Anderson's text includes his resumption after George Lawson intervened.
- The current Commons sitting page gives volume 698 for Eldon Griffiths.
  Both indexes and Historic Hansard give volume 696. The cited reference
  uses 696 and the row records the discrepancy.
- The older index assigns Patrick Duffy to 21 January 1964. The 8 July 1963
  Hansard transcript explicitly identifies his earlier address as his
  first. His by-election result supplies the Labour affiliation. The
  older entry is logged as superseded.
- Walter Alldritt's June 1964 swearing-in in Hansard establishes that he
  was sitting for Liverpool Scotland when he spoke in July. The available
  ParlParse membership history begins with his October term.
- Ronald Brown's 15 December 1964 contribution is listed in SN04588.
  SN04587 explicitly classifies it as a first mention and reports no
  speech. The candidate file preserves the transcript and both citations
  while leaving the maiden-speech classification unresolved.
